Before you start

LabLens is an educational guide, not a doctor. It compares your numbers with typical adult ranges and common patterns, then suggests possibilities and which kind of specialist to ask for. It cannot diagnose you or replace a medical examination, your symptoms, or your doctor’s judgement.

Always show your results to a qualified doctor before making decisions about treatment. Do not start, stop or change medicines because of this app.

Emergency? Chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, fainting, confusion, seizures, or sudden weakness: go to an emergency department now (Nigeria: call 112).
